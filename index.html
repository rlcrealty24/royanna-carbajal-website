About

A Broker's Precision. An Investor's Instinct.

Hi, I'm Royanna Carbajal — Founder & CEO of RLC Realty, broker, and investor. What began as a small real estate practice in South Florida has grown into a firm that helps clients buy, sell, rent, and build long-term wealth through property, now across two states.

My background is in business, accounting, and finance, and I bring that same precision to every transaction — the kind of attention that catches the details other people miss. It's paired with a personal, hands-on approach that South Florida clients have come to rely on, and one I'm now bringing to Colorado as well.

My goal is bigger than closing a transaction. Whether you're renting your first place, working to repair your credit before buying, relocating across state lines, or exploring your next investment, I'm here to guide you through it with care and clarity.

Wherever you're headed next, I'd be glad to help you get there.
