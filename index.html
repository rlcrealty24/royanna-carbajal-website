About

A Broker's Precision. An Investor's Instinct.

I'm Royanna Carbajal — Employing Broker, investor, and founder of RLC Realty, a boutique brokerage built around one standard: every client is treated like the only client.

With a background in business, accounting, and finance, I bring a strategist's discipline to every transaction, paired with a hands-on, relationship-driven approach that South Florida clients have come to expect — and one I'm now extending to Colorado as an Employing Broker.

Whether you're buying, selling, investing, or relocating across state lines, my role is the same: protect your interests, simplify the process, and make the outcome feel inevitable.
